What types of freight does Freight Nexus handle?

Intermodal container moves, bulk and bagged grain, and general freight — dry van, flatbed and reefer — across the United States, Canada and Mexico.

How fast can I get a quote?

Most quotes are returned within 15 minutes during business hours. Spot capacity is typically confirmed within two hours of acceptance.

Is my cargo insured?

Every load carries $250,000 in standard cargo coverage. Excess coverage is available for high-value shipments — just tell us when you request the quote.

Can I track my shipment in real time?

Yes. GPS updates every 30 seconds, milestone alerts by SMS and email, and a live dashboard on every load — no app to install.

Do you handle cross-border shipments?

We manage customs documentation, bonded carriers and broker coordination for Canada and Mexico lanes, including Laredo, Detroit and Blaine crossings.
